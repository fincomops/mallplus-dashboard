1 The stack — who owns what flows: advertiser → DSP → SSP → delivery, fueled by DMP

DMP · Data Management Platform
GCash
  • 94M registered users · 35M fully e-KYC'd
  • KYC demographics (age/gender/location/SEC)
  • Payment platform: banked vs unbanked · wallet vs bank-linked · card · GGives credit
  • Transaction behavior: banked/unbanked, high/low spender, send-money/bills/load activities
  • 50+ interest affinities (nightlifers, pet lovers, beauty junkies…)
  • City-level geo · GGives credit signals
SSP · Supply-Side Platform
GCash + Mall Plus
  • GCash surfaces: receipt page ₱230 · promo card ₱320 · mobile banner ₱300 · pop up ₱380 (MadTech 2026 real)
  • Mall Plus surfaces: Carousel ₱285 · Featured Brands ₱304 · Hot Keywords ₱11.40 CPC · Pop Up ₱361 (A-scenario, MadTech −5%) · Push TBD
  • Reservation mechanics: prepaid · 2-hr payment · guaranteed slots (Pop Up/FB) · 1,000-imp increments
  • Unified rate card = transparent CPM commitment (differentiation vs auctions)
DSP · Demand-Side Platform
Mall Plus
  • Campaign builder: objective → bidding mode → target level
  • Product mix — SKU/category lines with share & AOV (e.g., Electronics: smartphones 60% · laptops 40%)
  • Duration & flight plan — start/end dates · 14-day calibration freeze · daily pacing (₱/day, imps/day)
  • Targeting UI on DMP segments (broad → interest → transaction → CRM retarget)
  • Budget & ROI dials (Launch/Standard/Conservative; future Auto/GMV-Max style)
  • Managed tier (JBP 1–3, −5%/−10%) + future self-serve
  • Reporting: impressions fill, blended CPM, CTR, CPC, landing conv, ROAS

🚧 Operating rules

• Impressions guaranteed (CPM contract) — ROAS delivered, never promised.
• 14-day calibration freeze; changes restart the window.
• DMP data is GCash's moat — Mall Plus (DSP) licenses segments, doesn't own them.
• SSP neutrality: GCash surfaces priced at MadTech card; Mall Plus at −5% — the spread is the JBP discount headroom.
• Push Notification (candidate 5th) excluded until GCash quotes the rate.
• Model mirrors OptimO's "ready-made audiences" and TikTok's ROI-dial — automation is the upgrade path, not the launch.
