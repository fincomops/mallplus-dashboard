2 · Set the objective & bidding mode

  • Objective first (from client brief): Reach/brand (Carousel + Featured Brands + Pop Up) · Conversion (Hot Keywords) · Full-funnel (blended package).
  • Manual (launch default) — fixed rate-card rates: transparent, predictable, the Mall Plus differentiation vs opaque auctions.
  • Auto (upgrade path) — a future "Sponsored Max"-style Mall Plus package auto-allocates budget across the 4 formats toward a client ROAS target (validated by Lazada Sponsored Max, TikTok GMV Max, Shopee GMV Max).
  • Package tier sets the mix — Tier 1 Launch = Carousel 50% + Hot Keywords 30% + FB 20% → up to Imperial = Pop Up-heavy 50% (CPM-Showcase mix weights).

Benchmark rule (from Shopee): use historical performance as the starting ROAS/CPM target — for Mall Plus, anchor on Q2'23 GCash Ad Solutions CTRs (Pop Up 3.93% · Carousel 0.80% · Featured Brands 0.50% · Hot Kw 2.00%) until the Business Partner Solutions deck lands, then refresh.

4 · Launch & calibration window (14 days)

  • Every new campaign (or any change to rates, creatives, placements, keywords) starts a 14-day calibration window.
  • Expect fluctuations — do NOT adjust rates, swap creatives, or change placements mid-window (mirrors Shopee's learning-phase rule).
  • Client flag: "performance fluctuates during the first 14 days; changes restart the window from Day 0."
  • For reserved formats (Featured Brands, Pop Up), calibration applies to the next booking window, not live guaranteed slots.

6 · Monthly review & re-targeting

  1. Pull performance by format vs the previous month.
  2. Re-benchmark targets: update CTRs/CPMs/CPCs when the BPS deck CTRs arrive; refresh blended CPM if rates move.
  3. Re-target ROAS/CPM per client (Shopee recommends updating ROAS targets monthly).
  4. Tier migration: if budget crosses JBP thresholds (₱21–50M → −5%, >₱50M → −10% + free media), re-price the package and re-run the blended CPM.
  5. Decide: renew reserved slots (Pop Up / Featured Brands) next month — guaranteed slots are date-bookable and sell out.

7 · Guardrails & caveats

  • Impressions are guaranteed; ROAS is not — CPM contracts guarantee delivery (reserved slots), but sales depend on creative + landing page + offer. Never promise ROAS on a CPM product.
  • Learning-phase discipline — the 14-day freeze is the single biggest execution lever; mid-flight changes reset calibration and waste budget.
  • ROAS targets differ by format — Pop Up (3.93% CTR) outperforms browse formats; do not set one ROAS target across formats.
  • TBD items (from Shopee H1 2026): Homepage Brand Ads CPM, GMV Max rate, AMS commissions — cross-check with the AMS/KAM before external use; keep Mall Plus analogs marked TBD until validated.
  • Operational mirror: prepaid, 2-hour payment window, no refunds/transfers, unlimited slots — keep Mall Plus booking simple and Shopee-consistent.
