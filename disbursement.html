📖 How to Use the Disbursement Portal

1 Submitting a Request

Click + New Request on the My Requests tab. Fill in all required fields:

  • Vendor — name of the supplier or payee
  • Description — what the payment is for
  • Budget Line — select from dropdown (category is auto-filled)
  • Amount — amount to be paid (₱)
  • VAT Classification — VATable 12%, VAT-Exempt, Zero-Rated, or Non-VAT
  • Due Date — when payment should be released
  • Attachment — optional quote or invoice (drag & drop)

2 Approval Chain

  • Level 1: Budget owner of the selected budget line
  • Level 2 (Final): Shaun Ochia if amount < ₱5,000 / Patt Soyao if ≥ ₱5,000
  • If you are your own approver at a level, that level is automatically skipped

3 Budget Flags

If your request amount exceeds the remaining budget for the selected line, approvers will see a ⚠️ warning badge. Submission is still allowed — the flag is informational only.

4 Editing & Cancelling

  • Edit: Allowed while status is Pending (before first approval). Changes to amount or budget line re-resolve the approval chain.
  • Cancel: Allowed while Pending. Requires a reason. This is permanent.
  • Resubmit: Available after Rejected or Rejected by Finance. Starts a fresh approval cycle.

5 Finance Payment & Rejection

  • Finance team (Shaun, Emmaly, Fatima) sees all Approved requests in the Payments tab
  • Pay: marks as Paid and notifies the submitter
  • Reject for Payment: sends back to submitter with a reason (submitter can resubmit)
