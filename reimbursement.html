📝 Submitting a Reimbursement

Everything you need to know as a requester.

Getting Started

Registration (first time only): Click "Register" on the login screen. Provide your name, email, select your department, and create a PIN (4+ digits). Your department determines who reviews your request.

Login: Use your email and PIN. The system remembers you — no need to re-register.

Reimbursement Cycle & Deadlines

⏰ Bi-Weekly Schedule

Reimbursements are processed every two weeks, independent from payroll. Payments are sent via direct bank transfer.

📅 Submission CutoffWednesday at 5:00 PM (pay week)
🔍 Review & AuditThursday – Friday
💵 PayoutFriday (pay week)
⚠️ Late Submissions: Claims received after Wednesday 5:00 PM automatically roll over to the next cycle two weeks later. Submit early to avoid delays.

General Rules

  • Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date.
  • Combine all receipts from the previous two weeks into a single batch submission per cycle.
  • Itemized receipts are strictly required. Credit card slips or payment confirmations without a valid invoice are not accepted.
  • The Purpose field must clearly state the business justification for the expense.
  • The Finance Department reserves the right to reject a request even after initial approval if the submitted invoice is deemed unacceptable.

Go to the 📝 Submit Request tab and fill in:

Duplicate Detection

The system checks for duplicates in two ways:

  1. Identical receipt — same file already submitted before
  2. Matching fields — same vendor, same date, same amount

If a potential duplicate is found, you'll see a warning banner. You can still submit — just click Submit again.

Tracking Your Requests

Go to 📋 My Requests to see all your submissions. Each shows:

  • Reimbursement ID (e.g. RMB-20260730-0001)
  • Status flow: Pending → Pending Second → Pending Final → Approved / Rejected / Paid / Cancelled
  • While Pending you can ✏️ Edit or ✖ Cancel; if Rejected you can ✏️ Edit & Resubmit — see Editing, Cancelling & Resubmitting below.
  • Your request may go through 1–3 approval levels depending on your department and amount
  • Who approved it and when
  • Receipt link (📎 icon if you uploaded one)

The Finance team will notify you within 24 hours of review if your expense is flagged or needs clarification — giving you time to resolve before the Friday payout.

Editing, Cancelling & Resubmitting

🔒 Locked after the first approval

You can edit or cancel only while your request is Pending (before the first approval). Once an approver acts on it, the request is locked — if something is wrong, ask your approver to reject it, then fix and resubmit.

  • ✏️ Edit — while Pending, click Edit on your request in 📋 My Requests. You can change any field: purchase date, amount, category, vendor, VAT status, invoice/OR number, purpose, notes, or upload a replacement receipt. Changing the amount recalculates the approval routing — for example, crossing ₱5,000 adds an extra approval level.
  • ✖ Cancel — while Pending, click Cancel. A reason is required. Cancelling is permanent — the request can't be reopened, so submit a new request if you still need reimbursement. Your approvers are not notified when you cancel, so tell them directly if they need to know.
  • ✏️ Edit & Resubmit — if your request was Rejected (or Rejected Final), click Edit & Resubmit. It keeps the same reimbursement ID, returns to Pending, and restarts the approval flow from the beginning — approvers get a fresh notification.

✅ Reviewing & Processing Approvals

How to review and approve/reject requests. The approval flow is configurable — contact Finance if you have questions about your department's routing.

Who Approves What

Requests are routed based on the requester's department and amount. Each department has 1–3 approval levels:

> ₱5,000 Mhike or Jon → Justin → Patt

Multi-Level Approval Flow

Requests flow through up to three levels depending on department and amount:

  1. Level 1 (Pending) — First approver(s) review. Multiple approvers at one level = either/or.
  2. Level 2 (Pending Second) — If configured, advances to the next approver.
  3. Level 3 (Pending Final) — Final sign-off, typically for amounts above ₱5,000.

🔄 Self-Approval Blocked: You cannot approve your own requests. If you submit a reimbursement, you're automatically removed from your level's approver list and it skips to the next person.

Approving a Request

Go to ✅ Approvals tab. You'll see only requests awaiting your review at your current level.

  • Approve: Click "Approve." If there are more levels, the request advances (status changes to "Pending Second" or "Pending Final"). If you're the last level, it's fully Approved.
  • Reject: Click "Reject" and provide a reason. The requester will see why.

💡 Tip: The status badge shows which level the request is at. "Pending" = Level 1, "Pending Second" = Level 2, "Pending Final" = Level 3.

💵 Paying Approved Requests

Payment processing for Finance team members.

Bi-Weekly Payment Cycle

📅 Submission CutoffWednesday at 5:00 PM
🔍 Review & ProcessThursday – Friday
💵 PayoutFriday (direct bank transfer)

Payments are processed separately from payroll. Late submissions automatically roll over to the next cycle.

Payment Board

Shows all Approved requests ready for payment. You can filter by Department and VAT Status.

Columns: ID | Submit Date | Purchase Date | Employee | Email | Dept | Bank Account | Vendor | Invoice | VAT | Amount | Category | Approved By | Receipt | Duplicate Flag | Action

The Bank Account column auto-populates from the master bank account sheet. No manual entry needed.

Manual Pay (Single Request)

Click the 💵 Pay button on any row. A modal appears:

  1. Payment Date — defaults to today
  2. Payment Reference — e.g. check number, transfer ID, batch reference

This appends [Payment Ref: XXX] to the request notes and marks the status as Paid.

Reject for Payment

If a receipt is not acceptable or an error slipped past the approver's review, click ✗ Reject on the row.

  1. A rejection reason is required (e.g. receipt unacceptable — missing details, blurry, wrong entity)
  2. Status becomes Rejected by Finance — the requester and the approvers of record are emailed with your reason
  3. The requester can fix the issue and Edit & Resubmit, which starts a fresh approval cycle

Only Approved requests can be rejected. Paid requests are locked.

Batch Payment (CSV Upload)

For bulk processing, click 💳 Batch Payment Upload at the bottom of the Payments page.

CSV Format: Reimbursement ID, Payment Date, Reference

  1. Download the CSV Template (📥 button in the format instructions)
  2. Fill in the IDs, dates, and references
  3. Upload — the first row is auto-detected as a header if it doesn't start with "RMB-"
  4. Results show: Matched ✅ | Not Found ❌ | Already Paid ⚠️ | Wrong Status ⚠️
⚠️ Important: Only requests with Approved status are updated. Others are skipped.

CSV Export

Click 📥 Export CSV to download all approved-for-payment requests with bank account details, ready for upload to your banking platform.
