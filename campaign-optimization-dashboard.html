5 Guardrails & caveats

🎯 Impressions guaranteed
CPM contracts guarantee delivery (reserved slots). ROAS depends on creative + landing + offer — never promise ROAS on a CPM product.
⛔ 14-day freeze
Mid-flight changes reset calibration and waste budget. The single biggest execution lever.
📊 ROAS differs by format
Pop Up (3.93% CTR) outruns browse formats — never set one ROAS target across formats.
🔍 TBD items
Homepage Brand Ads CPM, GMV Max rate, AMS commissions — cross-check AMS/KAM before external use.
🔄 JBP migration
Budget crosses ₱21M or ₱50M → re-price with −5% / −10% + free media, re-run blended CPM.
🔔 Push = candidate 5th
GCash-official format (CPM-billed), rate TBD via Partner Marketing — keep excluded from blended CPM until confirmed.
