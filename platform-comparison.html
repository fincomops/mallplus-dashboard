06 · What this means for Mall Plus — five takeaways

How the competitive set sharpens the Mall Plus pitch, the rate card, and the package mechanics.
1
The model is validated — same two layers everywhere
Every platform runs auction performance on intent (search/browse) + premium CPM brand on owned high-attention surfaces. Mall Plus is not inventing a format — it's entering a proven shape with a better-owned surface: the transaction moment.
2
Grab's in-transit is the closest analog to Mall Plus' Pop Up
Post-transaction placement at 2.5× engagement, less competing content — Grab (a transaction-first superapp) structurally validates Mall Plus' receipt-page & Pop Up inventory. The GCash "in transit" is the payment confirmation screen.
3
Transparency is the differentiation
TikTok is auction-only (no rate card) and Grab doesn't publish feed/transit pricing. Mall Plus' published rate card — costed 5% below the MadTech card (₱285–361 CPM + ₱11.40 CPC) — is a fixed, predictable commitment, a pitch advantage for budget planners who hate opaque auctions.
4
Reserved & guaranteed premium slots are market standard
Shopee (Homepage Brand Ads), Lazada (Sponsored Display) and Grab (Masthead) all sell premium as date-bookable, guaranteed inventory. This validates the Mall Plus package unlocks + JBP tier-discount reservation model (₱21–50M −5% · >₱50M −10%).
5
Keep it self-serve-able, sell the premium managed
All four platforms publish self-serve ad managers. Mall Plus' 4-format lineup is already simple enough for self-serve booking (Carousel / Featured Brands / Hot Keywords) with premium Pop Up sold managed — matching how Shopee & Grab split self-serve vs AMS/GrabAds-team.
